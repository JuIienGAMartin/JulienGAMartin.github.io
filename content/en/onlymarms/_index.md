## How it started

We created an account on on [OnlyFans](https://onlyfans.com) called [OnlyMarms](https://onlyfans.com/onlymarms) to hopefully get some donations to support our research, but also to maybe attract some media attention and reach ou to the public in a new and creative way.

Following sever budget cuts in research and science in general in the US, our long-term research project started to be threatened by lack of funding. After watching the hit show "Margot got money troubles" Pr Daniel Blumstein said maybe we should to an OnlyFans account, ... but G-rated with marmot content only. We all agreed and after some discussions onwhat to publish and struggles to get approved by OnlyFans, here we are.

The [OnlyMarms](https://onlyfans.com/onlymarms) account attracted some donations but what we never expected was the amount of media attention we got. It went viral fast. Here is but a subset on media coverage we got and interviews we gave (a simple search "onlymarms on onlyfans" on the web retrieve hundreds of appropriate links from media coverage throughout the world)

## The meme coin

It has been a crazy ride since we started [OnlyMarms](https://onlyfans.com/onlymarms). The media attention has been intense and worldwide.
The meme coin community discovered and loved the story so much that they decided to create a meme coin based on [Solana blockchain](https://solana.com/) called [$ONLYMARMS](https://onlymarmssolana.org/). The coin is now available on [pump.fun](https://pump.fun/coin/HBrfYZgeLKdSvBBGnGkvAK4563pq8oBGpgNFAaespump) and has already raised over $90K in donations for marmot research efforts.

The $ONLYMARMS coin is a fun and creative way to support marmot conservation and research. The funds raised will go directly to the Yellow-bellied Marmot Research Project, which is dedicated to studying marmots in their natural habitats.

The $ONLYMARMS coin is a testament to the power of community and the love for marmots. We are grateful for the support and enthusiasm from the meme coin community, and we look forward to seeing how this unique initiative will continue to grow and make a positive impact on marmot conservation.
